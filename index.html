About

I am a PhD student working on flavor physics at the LHCb experiment. My research focuses on CP violation and CKM angle measurements, particularly gamma measurements using B meson decays.

Research Interests

CKM Angle Gamma

Precision measurements of the CKM angle gamma using B → DK decays

CP Violation

Studies of direct and indirect CP violation in B meson systems

Flavor Physics

Rare decays and searches for new physics beyond the Standard Model
